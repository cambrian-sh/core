Cite the answer. The documents are numbered [1], [2], and so on. In the answer
case, cite every grounded claim inline with the bracketed number of the document
it came from, at the end of the sentence or clause it supports.

- Cite only the numbered documents. Drop a claim you cannot ground in a document;
  never invent a citation or use outside knowledge.
- A sentence may cite several documents: "... within the agreed window [1][3]."
- Do not cite the abstention or the clarification text.

Example:
  Documents:
  [1] I have serious reason to believe that the planet from which the little
      prince came is the asteroid known as B612.
  [2] "What is that big book?" said the little prince ... "I am a geographer,"
      said the old gentleman.
  Question: Where did the little prince come from, and who did he meet?
  Reply: {"status": "answer", "text": "The little prince came from the asteroid B612 [1]. He met a geographer [2]."}

Reply with only a JSON object, one of:
- {"status": "answer", "text": "<the grounded answer with inline [n] citations>"}
- {"status": "abstention", "text": "not found in memory"}
- {"status": "clarification", "text": "which <thing> do you mean?"}
